# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-07-23

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Siamrooftech installs retractable awnings for homes, restaurants, offices, and businesses in Bangkok and nearby provinces.

**What it does:** Siamrooftech sells and installs retractable awnings, with the highest commercial priority on manual and electric retractable awning systems. The site should position the business around professional site assessment, installation quality, real project proof, and after-sales confidence rather than cheap DIY awnings.

**Product category:** กันสาดพับเก็บได้, กันสาดพับได้, กันสาดพับไฟฟ้า, กันสาดไฟฟ้า, กันสาดอัตโนมัติ, กันสาดมือหมุน

**Product type:** Local service business / installation contractor.

**Business model:** Project-based installation work. Pricing depends on site dimensions, fabric/material, manual vs electric system, and installation conditions. Exact conversion flow is not finalized yet.

## Target Audience
**Target companies/customers:** Homeowners, restaurants/cafes, companies, and offices that need shade/rain protection without permanent fixed roofing.

**Decision-makers:** Homeowners, restaurant/cafe owners, office/business owners, facility/admin managers.

**Primary use case:** Customers want a retractable awning that can block sun/rain when needed and fold away when they want open space, appearance, or flexibility.

**Jobs to be done:**
- Create shade and rain protection for usable outdoor/frontage space.
- Improve comfort and appearance without building a permanent roof.
- Get a professionally measured and installed awning that fits the real site.

**Use cases:**
- Residential terrace, balcony, side yard, parking, or front-of-house shade.
- Restaurant/cafe frontage where shade affects seating comfort and customer experience.
- Office/company outdoor area, entrance, or staff/customer waiting zone.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Customers need shade/rain protection but do not want a permanent, bulky, or visually heavy roof.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Fixed roofing can make the space feel dark, permanent, or overbuilt.
- Cheap marketplace/DIY awnings may not match the site and may create installation risk.
- Generic contractors may not specialize in retractable awnings.

**What it costs them:** Less usable outdoor space, hotter frontage, lower comfort for family/customers/staff, and risk of paying for an awning that does not fit the site.

**Emotional tension:** Customers want the area solved cleanly, but worry about price uncertainty, weak materials, poor workmanship, and whether the installed awning will look good.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Other retractable-awning specialists and awning installers ranking for "กันสาดพับเก็บได้", "กันสาดพับไฟฟ้า", and "กันสาดอัตโนมัติ".

**Secondary:** Large home improvement retailers, marketplaces, and DIY awning sellers that compete on price and convenience but do not necessarily provide site-specific installation expertise.

**Indirect:** Fixed roof contractors, metal sheet awning providers, polycarbonate roof providers, and general canvas/tarpaulin shops.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Focus on retractable awnings as the money product, especially "กันสาดพับเก็บได้" and "กันสาดพับไฟฟ้า".
- Real installation portfolio with homes, cafes/restaurants, offices, hotels, and other sites.
- Proof assets exist or should be collected: years of experience, number of installations, corporate customers, reviews, before/after photos, warranty, and in-house team.
- Service coverage across Bangkok and key nearby provinces.

**How we do it differently:** Lead with site-specific recommendation and real installed work rather than commodity product listings.

**Why that's better:** The buyer can see how the awning performs and looks in comparable real sites before contacting.

**Why customers choose us:** They need a contractor who understands retractable awnings, can assess the real site, install professionally, and provide after-sales confidence.

| "ราคาสูงกว่าสินค้าออนไลน์หรือ DIY" | The service includes site fit, installation, materials/system selection, and reduced installation risk. Compete on installed outcome, not bare product price. |
| "ไม่รู้ว่าควรเลือกระบบมือหมุนหรือไฟฟ้า" | Use site dimensions, usage frequency, storefront/home needs, and budget to recommend the right system. |
| "กลัวติดตั้งแล้วไม่แข็งแรงหรือไม่สวย" | Use real portfolio, before/after photos, warranty, and clear installation process as proof. |

**Anti-persona:** Jobs passed through by other contractors are not a target, even if search volume exists.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Heat, rain, unusable outdoor/frontage space, fixed roof feels too permanent, existing awning/roof does not solve the problem.

**Pull:** Retractable control, cleaner appearance, manual/electric options, professional installation, real examples.

**Habit:** Customers may delay, ask general contractors, buy cheaper marketplace products, or accept fixed-roof solutions.

**Anxiety:** Price uncertainty, whether the structure can support installation, durability, wind/rain performance, warranty, and after-sales support.

## SEO Guardrails
- Do not create doorway pages or thin location pages.
- Do not target contractor-pass-through work.
- Do not create mass pages before proving real service-area intent and unique local/project value.
- Every SEO page should map to a real service, real area, real proof, or real customer question.
- Use SSG/ISR for public pages. Do not add client-side rendering to public SEO pages unless technically unavoidable.
- Prioritize proof: real project photos, dimensions, area, customer type, system type, material, warranty, and installation notes.
